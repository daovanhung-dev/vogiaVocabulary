## Brand & Style

This design system embodies an energetic, optimistic, and academically sound educational environment designed for spontaneous multilingual vocabulary acquisition. It pairs rigorous linguistic pedagogical principles with delightful tactile gamification. The visual language avoids childish clutter and over-saturated juvenile tropes in favor of an airy, warm, editorial-meets-playful atmosphere: confident, welcoming, clean, and meticulously crafted.

Key brand characteristics:
- **Warm & Scholarly:** Grounded in soft alabaster and cream paper tones, conveying focus, comfort, and timeless study habits rather than stark tech clinicality.
- **Vibrant & Tactile:** Micro-interactions feature subtle spring physics, pill badges, and layered depth that make language discovery feel rewarding and kinetic.
- **Unobtrusive & Accessible:** High-contrast slate typography meets WCAG AA standards, with an open guest-first architecture that foregoes authentication gates and onboarding friction.
- **Signature Mascot ("Lexi"):** A soft mint-teal language sprite styled as an expressive, rounded conversational bubble with curious dark slate eyes and a warm golden sparkle star antenna, guiding drills without patronizing the learner.

## Colors

The palette balances warm organic foundations with crisp oceanic teals and punchy gamification accents:

- **Canvas & Surfaces:**
  - Base canvas: `#FAF8F5` (Soft Alabaster / Warm Cream).
  - Subtle inset/recessed containers: `#F5F1EB`.
  - Elevated cards & interactives: `#FFFFFF`.
- **Primary Spectrum (Teal & Cyan):**
  - Anchor Teal: `#0D9488` (interactive primaries, strong mastery states).
  - Bright Mint-Teal: `#14B8A6` (active toggles, hover strokes, mascot highlights).
  - Horizon Cyan: `#0284C7` (secondary pathways, audio pronunciation triggers).
- **Gamification & Feedback Accents:**
  - Vitality Coral: `#F43F5E` / `#FB7185` (streak flames, error correction, retention alerts).
  - Amber & Gold: `#F59E0B` / `#FBBF24` / `#EAB308` (XP points, star ratings, combo streaks).
  - Success Herb: `#10B981` (correct translations, completed review decks).
- **Ink & Boundaries:**
  - Deep Navy Slate: `#0F172A` (titles, high-emphasis vocabulary heads).
  - Reading Slate: `#1E293B` and `#334155` (body text, definitions, IPA transcriptions).
  - Muted Slate: `#64748B` (etymology notes, meta tags, shortcut keys).
  - Structural Borders: `#E2E8F0` and warm edge `#EFEBE4`.

## Typography

The design system standardizes on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all tiers to achieve a contemporary, geometric, yet gently radiused look that supports high legibility during repetitive speed-drills and linguistic study.

- **Display & Target Terms (`headline-xl`, `headline-lg`):** Reserved for foreign flashcard targets, master word challenges, and chapter overviews. Letter tracking is slightly tightened (`-0.02em`) for a cohesive display impact.
- **IPA & Pronunciation Subheads (`headline-sm`, `body-lg`):** Distinct letter clarity allows learners to immediately differentiate phonetic nuances and accents.
- **Micro-Labels & Keyboard Indicators (`label-sm`, `label-md`):** Rendered with crisp semi-bold or bold weights, upper-case letter-spacing (`+0.04em`), serving quick-press flashcard numbers, grammar categorization badges (noun, verb, idiom), and anonymous session state tags.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy centers on a focused, single-task cognitive experience. It avoids multi-sidebar cockpit layouts during study sessions in favor of centered, progressive discovery columns:

- **Desktop (min-width: 1024px):** Max container width of `840px` for vocabulary practice interfaces; `1120px` for course path selection and multi-language dashboards. 12-column grid with `1.5rem` gutters and dynamic centered margins.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout with `1.5rem` margin and gutters. Floating persistent bottom or side controls consolidate into unified command docks.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** Single-column stacked layout with `1rem` margins. The primary study card occupies the golden ratio of viewport height, keeping answer pads and audio buttons within easy thumb reach.
- **Session Bar (Non-gated UX):** A slim `3rem` sticky top bar that displays the active target language pill, local streak count, and an auto-saving anonymous storage indicator ("Stored locally").

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy leverages warm ambient shadows and tonal surface stacking rather than stark dark drop shadows:

- **Base Canvas:** Flat `#FAF8F5` base plane.
- **Surface Elevation 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure `#FFFFFF` surfaces with subtle border `1px solid #EFEBE4` and a dual warm shadow: `0 1px 3px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04), 0 4px 12px rgba(245, 158, 11, 0.03)`.
- **Surface Elevation 2 (Active Learning Card / Focused State):** Slightly raised with `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(13, 148, 136, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.04)`.
- **Interactive Depth (Tactile Buttons):** Primary action buttons utilize a 3D tactile bottom bevel edge (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0 #0F766E` for teal buttons) that translates down `2px` on active press, creating physical satisfaction upon completing reviews.

## Shapes

The design uses deeply rounded, organic geometries to echo speech bubbles, open dialogues, and approachable learning tools:

- **Pills (`rounded-full`):** Category labels, grammar badges, language switches, audio replay capsules, and streak indicators.
- **Cards & Modal Trays:** Master flashcards use `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem`) on mobile and `rounded-3xl` (`1.75rem`) on desktop, eliminating severe corners and reducing cognitive stress.
- **Feedback Chits:** Correct/incorrect toast capsules use `rounded-2xl` with prominent border strokes for tactile recognition.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary (Mastery / Continue):** Solid `#0D9488` background with `#0F766E` 3D bottom bevel (`box-shadow: 0 4px 0`). White bold text. Hover shifts to `#14B8A6`. Active click depresses by `2px` with bevel reducing to `2px`.
- **Secondary (Audio / Hint / Skip):** Warm alabaster background (`#F5F1EB`), slate text (`#334155`), border `1.5px solid #E2E8F0`. Hover triggers a mint stroke tint and subtle lift.
- **Choice Buttons (Multiple Choice Drills):** Full-width rounded cards (`rounded-xl`), `#FFFFFF` background, `2px solid #E2E8F0`. On keyboard hotkey or click: transforms to teal border with soft teal tint (`#F0FDFA`). Upon answer reveal: converts to success emerald or error coral.

### Chips & Badges
- **Part of Speech Pills:** Compact uppercase badges with light backgrounds matching grammatical types (e.g., Verb in soft teal `#CCFBF1`, Noun in warm amber `#FEF3C7`, Adjective in gentle sky `#E0F2FE`).
- **Streak & XP Counter:** Warm pill featuring a high-contrast coral/amber flame icon, bold navy number, and faint golden border.

### Input Fields & Spelling Tiles
- **Spelling Fill-in-the-Blank:** Standalone segmented character boxes or clean centered inputs with large typography (`headline-md`), border-bottom `3px solid #CBD5E1`. Focused state turns vibrant teal with a subtle pulsing focus ring.

### Flashcards & Study Modules
- **Interactive Vocabulary Canvas:** Centered, double-sided rounded card container with minimal decorative clutter. Front contains target word in primary teal/navy, IPA phonetic label, audio pronunciation button, and subtle context sentence. Back contains grammatical breakdown, etymology breadcrumbs, and native translation.

### Anonymous Session & Progress Tracker
- **No-Auth Progress Badge:** Positioned discreetly in the top header. Shows an offline-ready device badge ("Local Session") with a soft pulsing mint dot, signaling that retention data and custom wordbanks persist safely in local client storage.
